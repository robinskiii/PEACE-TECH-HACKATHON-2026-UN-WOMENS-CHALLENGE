Women candidates face wave of online abuse ahead of city vote

By a staff reporter · Iloilo City

With two weeks until the city election, three women running for council say they have received a sharp rise in abusive messages online, according to a local monitoring group.

Mayor Liza Reyes, who is seeking a second term, said her office had recorded dozens of posts attacking her appearance and family life since she announced her port redevelopment plan.

"Criticize my budget, that's democracy. But when they say a mother cannot run a city, that is not about policy," Reyes told reporters on Tuesday.

Opposition councilor Dante Villanueva said the port plan was rushed and that the environmental review should be reopened before any contracts are signed.

In an op-ed shared widely on social media last week, a columnist wrote that women are too emotional for executive office and that the city needs a strong man to lead it, a claim researchers say has no basis.

The monitoring group urged residents not to share abusive posts, even to condemn them, and to report them to the platforms instead.
